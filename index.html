How pricing works

Each service line: quantity × your rate × condition multiplier × access multiplier (stories multiplier applies to house and roof lines). The cost engine separately computes what the job truly costs you:

  • Wash time = quantity ÷ production rate, slowed by condition (moderate ×1.25 time, heavy ×1.6 time), plus 15 minutes setup per line.
  • Labor = total hours × crew size × your labor rate.
  • Chemical = mix gallons (per 1,000 units) at an SH strength set by condition — light 0.8%, moderate 1.2%, heavy 2.0% — plus surfactant.
  • Plus equipment wear per hour, flat fuel, travel miles, and the tank-fill fee/time when there's no spigot.

Break-even = true cost + overhead. Recommended prices to your target margin. Premium = recommended + 15%.

The profit floor

Your floor is a minimum margin (default 30%) on the final price. If you type a final price below the floor price, GrimeMath shows a red warning and will not save the quote until you either raise the price or tick the one-time override box and own the decision. The floor price also never drops below your minimum job charge. This is the guardrail that stops "busy but broke" jobs.

Localize the default rates — important

The pre-loaded rates (house $0.22/sq ft, driveway $0.20/sq ft, roof $0.45/sq ft, etc.) are national planning defaults inside the published $0.15–$0.60/sq ft bands — they are starting points, not your market's prices. Before quoting real work: check what 3–5 local competitors charge, set your rates and production speeds to match YOUR rig and YOUR market in Settings, and re-check after 10 jobs against your actual times. A calculator with wrong inputs gives confidently wrong prices.

Guided measures & voice entry

Guided Measures (top of New Quote) does the measuring math for you: enter a rectangle's length × width for a driveway, deck, patio or flatwork and tap "Use this area"; enter a house's length, width and wall height and the perimeter estimator works out the washable soft-wash square footage; or build a fence run from sections × length per section and get the total linear feet (and the washable surface, both sides counted when you tick the box). Each result drops into the matching service line and is priced exactly as if you typed it.

Voice entry: tap the microphone button beside any text or number field and say the value — for a number field, say it in words ("twenty by forty" won't fit one field, so say "twenty" in the length field and "forty" in the width field, or "eight hundred" for a quantity). The words land in the field as if typed; nothing is saved or sent until you tap the usual buttons. Where voice isn't available on your device or browser, the microphone shows a short note and typing works as always.
